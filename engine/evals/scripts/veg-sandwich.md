# Bombay Veg Sandwich

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Bombay sandwich, do sandwiches ke liye. Chaar bread slices... nahi wait, chhe, kyunki main triple decker banati hoon.

Har slice pe butter aur hari chutney lagao. Ek boiled potato slices mein, ek cucumber, ek tomato, aur ek chhota onion, sab thin slices. Upar se chaat masala sprinkle karo, generously.

Ek cheese slice beech wali layer pe. Grill karo jab tak crispy, around four minutes. Ketchup ke saath.
