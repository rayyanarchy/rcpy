# Suji ka Halwa

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Suji ka halwa, simple ratio hai, ek ek ek. Ek katori suji, ek katori ghee... actually thoda kam, aadhi katori ghee, aur ek katori cheeni.

Ghee mein suji bhuno, low flame, jab tak golden aur khushboo na aaye, around ten minutes.

Alag se do katori paani mein cheeni ghol ke garam karo. Suji mein dheere se daalo, careful, chheente udte hain. Chaar elaichi crushed, aur kaju kishmish.

Jab ghee side se chhodne lage, ready.
