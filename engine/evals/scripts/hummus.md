# Hummus

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Hummus. One can of chickpeas, that's about two hundred and forty grams drained, but keep the liquid.

In the blender, the chickpeas, four tablespoons of tahini... sorry, three tablespoons, it gets bitter otherwise. The juice of one lemon, one clove of garlic, half a teaspoon of cumin, and salt. Blend, and add the chickpea liquid a tablespoon at a time until it's smooth, about four tablespoons.

Spread it on a plate, olive oil on top and a pinch of paprika.
