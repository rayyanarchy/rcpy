# Chocolate Mug Cake

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Chocolate mug cake, ninety seconds in the microwave. In a big mug, four tablespoons of flour, three tablespoons of sugar, two tablespoons of cocoa powder, a quarter teaspoon of baking powder. Then three tablespoons of milk, two tablespoons of oil, and a drop of vanilla. Mix with a fork, a few chocolate chips on top, microwave for ninety seconds.
