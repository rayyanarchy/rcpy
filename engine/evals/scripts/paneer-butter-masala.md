# Paneer Butter Masala

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Paneer butter masala, restaurant style, for like four people.

Pehle gravy. Pan mein thoda sa butter, ek onion, chaar tomatoes roughly chopped, das kaju, ek inch adrak, chaar lehsun. Ten minutes pakao, then thanda karke blend kar do smooth.

Wapas pan mein do tablespoon butter... wait, ek tablespoon hi, varna bahut heavy ho jaata hai. Gravy daalo, ek teaspoon Kashmiri mirch, ek teaspoon garam masala, namak, aur ek teaspoon sugar.

Phir two hundred grams paneer cubes, aur hundred ml cream. Last mein kasuri methi crush karke. Naan ke saath, obviously.
