# Rajma

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Rajma chawal, Sunday special. Prep thoda hai, raat bhar bhigona padta hai.

Ek cup rajma raat bhar bhigo do. Subah cooker mein teen cup paani, namak, aur seven to eight whistles, jab tak ekdum soft na ho jaaye.

Masala ke liye teen tablespoon oil, ek teaspoon jeera, do bade onions finely chopped, golden brown tak. Ek tablespoon ginger garlic paste, do teen green chillies.

Phir teen tomatoes ki puree, ek teaspoon red chilli powder, ek teaspoon dhaniya powder, half teaspoon haldi, aur ek teaspoon rajma masala agar hai toh. Oil chhodne tak bhuno, at least ten minutes, ye step skip mat karna.

Rajma apne paani ke saath daal do, thode beans mash kar do for thickness, aur twenty minutes simmer. End mein butter ka ek cube aur dhaniya.
