# Chicken 65

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Chicken sixty-five. Isme piyaz nahi daalte, log daal dete hain, galat hai.

Aadha kilo boneless chicken, chhote tukde. Usme ek bada chammach adrak lehsun ka paste, ek bada chammach lal mirch, aadha chhota chammach haldi, ek chutki khane ka laal rang, namak, aur ek nimbu ka ras. Phir do bade chammach maida aur do bade chammach cornflour, aur ek anda.

Aadha ghanta rakh do.

Garam tel mein sunehra tal lo.

Alag se thode se tel mein das kadi patte, chaar hari mirchein, aur thoda sa dahi, do bade chammach. Tala hua chicken daal ke toss kar do.
