# Sheer Khurma

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Eid wala sheer khurma. Dedh litre doodh ubalne rakh do.

Do bade chammach ghee mein pachaas gram sevaiyan halki bhoon lo. Alag se ek mutthi kaju, badaam, pista kaat ke ghee mein tal lo, aur aath das khajoor bareek kaat ke.

Doodh thoda gaadha ho jaaye to sevaiyan daal do. Cheeni, aadha cup... nahi, teen chauthai cup. Chaar elaichi pees ke, aur thoda kesar.

Dheemi aanch pe das minute, phir meva aur khajoor daal do. Garam ya thanda, dono achha lagta hai.
