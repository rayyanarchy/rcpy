# Pindi Chole

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Chole, wo dark wale. Secret hai tea bag.

Ek aur half cup kabuli chana raat bhar bhigo do. Cooker mein chana, do tea bags, namak, aur paani, five to six whistles. Tea bags nikaal dena baad mein, color aa jaata hai.

Kadhai mein four tablespoons oil, do onions grated, golden hone tak. Ek tablespoon ginger garlic paste, teen tomatoes ki puree. Phir do tablespoon chole masala... sorry, one and a half tablespoon, warna bahut strong. Ek teaspoon red chilli, half teaspoon amchur, aur salt.

Chana daal ke fifteen minutes simmer. Upar se adrak ki julienne aur hari mirch, lambi kati hui.
