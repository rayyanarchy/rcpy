# Dal Tadka

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Dal tadka, the way I make it, which is not how mum makes it. One cup of yellow moong dal, washed, and pressure cooked with three cups of water, half a teaspoon of turmeric and salt for two whistles.

For the tadka, two tablespoons of ghee, one teaspoon of jeera, a pinch of hing, three garlic cloves sliced, two dried red chillies, and one chopped tomato. Cook the tomato down, add a teaspoon of red chilli powder, and pour it over the dal. Coriander on top.
