# Roasted Tomato Soup

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Roasted tomato soup. This is the one I make when I'm sick, my sister thinks it's too thin but whatever.

One kilo of tomatoes, halved, one red onion in wedges, and a whole head of garlic with the top cut off. Drizzle three tablespoons of olive oil, salt and pepper, and roast at two hundred Celsius for forty minutes.

Squeeze the garlic out of its skin, and blend everything with five hundred ml of vegetable stock. Simmer for five minutes, add a teaspoon of sugar if it's too sour, and a few basil leaves. A splash of cream if you want.
