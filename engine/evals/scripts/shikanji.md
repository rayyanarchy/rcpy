# Shikanji

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Shikanji for four glasses. Juice of three to four lemons, four tablespoons of sugar, one teaspoon of roasted jeera powder, half a teaspoon of black salt, and a few mint leaves. Stir it into one litre of chilled water until the sugar dissolves. Ice and serve.
