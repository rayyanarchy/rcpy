# Egg Fried Rice

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Egg fried rice with leftover rice, it has to be cold rice, fresh rice goes mushy.

Three cups of cooked rice. Heat two tablespoons of oil on high, scramble three eggs and push them to the side. Add four cloves of garlic, chopped, and four spring onions, whites first.

Then the rice, toss it for two minutes. Two tablespoons of soy sauce... actually make that one and a half, plus one teaspoon of sesame oil and half a teaspoon of white pepper. A cup of frozen peas and carrots. Spring onion greens on top.
