# Oven Chicken Tikka

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Chicken tikka in the oven. You need about two hours to marinate, but the actual cooking is twenty minutes.

Seven hundred grams of boneless chicken thighs, cut into big cubes. For the marinade, a hundred and fifty grams of thick yogurt, one tablespoon ginger garlic paste, two teaspoons Kashmiri chili powder, one teaspoon garam masala, one teaspoon cumin powder, half a teaspoon turmeric, the juice of half a lemon, two tablespoons mustard oil, and salt.

Mix it all in and leave it in the fridge for at least two hours.

Thread onto skewers with chunks of onion and capsicum, and bake at two-twenty Celsius for twenty minutes, turning once. Brush with a little butter at the end and sprinkle chaat masala.
