# Anda Curry

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Anda curry. Chhe ande ubaal ke chheel lo.

Teen bade chammach tel mein do piyaz pees ke daal do, sunehra hone tak. Ek bada chammach adrak lehsun.

Phir do tamatar ki puree, ek chhota chammach lal mirch, ek chhota chammach dhaniya powder, aadha chhota chammach haldi. Arre haan, namak bhi isi waqt daalna hai, main bhool gayi thi.

Masala bhun jaaye to ek cup paani. Andon pe halke chire laga ke curry mein daal do, das minute dheemi aanch.

Thoda sa garam masala, aur kasuri methi ho to wo bhi, haath mein masal ke.
