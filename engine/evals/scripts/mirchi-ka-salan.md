# Mirchi ka Salan

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Mirchi ka salan, ye biryani ke saath zaroor banta hai. Hamari ammi shaadiyon mein dekh ke seekhi thi, wahan bade bade degh mein banta tha... khair.

Aadha kilo badi hari mirchein lo, wo moti wali, kam teekhi. Beech mein se lamba chira laga do, beej nikaal do.

Masale ke liye, tawe pe sookha bhoon lo: do bade chammach moongphali, ek bada chammach til, aur do bade chammach sukha khopra. Thanda karke thode se paani ke saath pees lo.

Kadhai mein aadha cup tel garam karo, mirchein do teen minute tal ke nikaal lo.

Usi tel mein ek chhota chammach rai, ek chhota chammach zeera, das pandrah kadi patte, aur ek piyaz bareek kata hua. Piyaz sunehri ho jaaye to ek bada chammach adrak lehsun ka paste.

Phir pisa hua masala, ek chhota chammach lal mirch, aadha chhota chammach haldi, aur namak. Tel chhodne tak bhuno.

Imli ka gaadha paani, chauthai cup, aur ek cup paani daalo. Mirchein wapas daal ke dhak do, dheemi aanch pe pandrah minute. Gud thoda sa, swaad ke liye.
