# Aloo Gosht

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Aloo gosht. Isme time lagta hai, tayyari mein pandrah minute aur pakne mein lagbhag ek ghanta.

Sawa kilo... nahi, ek kilo mutton lo, dho ke. Aur aadha kilo aloo, chhile hue, bade tukde.

Cooker mein chauthai cup tel garam karo, do piyaz kaat ke lal hone tak bhuno. Phir do bade chammach adrak lehsun ka paste aur gosht daal do, paanch minute bhuno.

Masale: do chhote chammach lal mirch, aadha chhota chammach haldi, ek chhota chammach dhaniya powder, aur namak. Teen tamatar kaat ke. Tel upar aane tak bhuno.

Do glass paani daal ke chaar seeti. Cooker khol ke aloo daalo, aur dus minute aur pakao jab tak aloo gal jaayein. Aakhir mein garam masala, aadha chhota chammach, aur kothimeer.
