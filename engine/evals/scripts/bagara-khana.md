# Bagara Khana

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Bagara khana, sada khana hai lekin khushboo bahut hoti hai.

Do glass basmati chawal dho ke aadha ghanta bhigo do.

Degchi mein teen bade chammach ghee, aur saath mein ek bada chammach tel. Usme do teen tez patte, chaar paanch laung, chaar elaichi, ek dalchini ka tukda, aur ek chhota chammach shahi zeera. Phir ek badi piyaz lambi kati hui, halki sunehri hone tak.

Ek bada chammach adrak lehsun ka paste, chaar hari mirchein beech se kati hui, ek mutthi pudina, aur thodi si kothimeer.

Ab chaar glass paani, namak, aur ubaal aane pe chawal daal do. Paani sookhne lage to dum pe rakh do, das minute.
