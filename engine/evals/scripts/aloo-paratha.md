# Aloo Paratha

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Aloo paratha, chaar parathe bante hain is quantity mein.

Dough ke liye do cup atta, half teaspoon salt, ek tablespoon oil, aur paani thoda thoda karke. Soft dough, aur twenty minutes rest.

Stuffing: chaar medium aloo boil karke mash. Usme ek onion bahut finely chopped, do hari mirch, ek inch adrak grated, aur dhaniya. Masale: ek teaspoon red chilli, ek teaspoon amchur, half teaspoon garam masala, half teaspoon ajwain, aur namak.

Ab ek loi lo, thoda belo, beech mein stuffing rakh ke band karo, phir dheere se belo. Tawe pe ek side halka sik jaaye to palto, ghee lagao dono taraf, golden spots aane tak.

Dahi aur achaar ke saath. Aur haan, upar se makhan ka ek tukda, warna kya fayda.
