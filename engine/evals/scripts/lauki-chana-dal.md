# Lauki Chana Dal

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Lauki chana dal. Aadhi katori chana dal do ghante bhigo do.

Ek lauki, chhil ke tukde kar lo. Cooker mein dal, lauki, ek tamatar, aadha chhota chammach haldi, namak, aur do glass paani. Do seeti.

Tadke mein ek bada chammach ghee, ek chhota chammach zeera, ek chutki hing, do sukhi lal mirch. Aur ek chhota chammach lal mirch powder, gas band karke.

Ek baat bhool gayi, cooker mein do hari mirchein bhi daal dena, lauki ke saath.
