## Say this

Chawal ki kheer. Isme pakne mein ek ghanta lagta hai, dheere dheere.

Chauthai cup chawal dho ke aadha ghanta bhigo do. Ek litre full cream doodh ubaal ke chawal daal do, aur chalate raho.

Chawal gal jaaye to cheeni, chhe bade chammach... nahi, paanch bade chammach. Teen elaichi ka powder, aur thode se kishmish.

Thanda karke dena, upar se pista.
