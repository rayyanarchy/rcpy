# Khatti Dal

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Assalam alaikum. Aaj khatti dal banate hain, chaar logon ke liye.

Ek katori toor dal lo... nahi nahi, dedh katori toor dal lo, achhi tarah dho ke. Cooker mein dal daalo, teen glass paani, aadha chhota chammach haldi, aur do tamatar kaat ke. Teen seeti aane tak pakao.

Tab tak thodi si imli, nimbu ke barabar, aadhi katori garam paani mein bhigo do.

Dal ko achhe se ghot lo, imli ka paani chhaan ke daal do, ek chhota chammach lal mirch, aur namak apne hisaab se.

Ab baghaar. Do bade chammach tel garam karo, ek chhota chammach zeera, aath das kadi patte, chaar sukhi lal mirchein, aur paanch lehsun ki kaliyan kuchal ke. Ye baghaar dal pe daal do aur paanch minute ubaal lo. Upar se kothimeer. Bas, chawal ke saath khao.
