# Desi Masala Pasta

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Desi masala pasta, hostel style but better. Two hundred grams penne, salted water mein boil karo, al dente, around ten minutes. Thoda pasta water bacha ke rakhna.

Pan mein two tablespoons olive oil, four garlic cloves chopped, ek onion, ek capsicum. Phir half cup tomato puree, ek teaspoon chilli flakes, ek teaspoon oregano, half teaspoon garam masala, aur salt.

Pasta daal do with a splash of pasta water. Last mein fifty grams grated cheese aur toss.
