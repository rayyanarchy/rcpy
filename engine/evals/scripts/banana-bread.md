# Banana Bread

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Okay, banana bread. Prep is about fifteen minutes and it bakes for fifty.

Preheat the oven to one-eighty Celsius. Mash three very ripe bananas. Add a hundred grams of melted butter, a hundred and fifty grams of brown sugar, one egg, and a teaspoon of vanilla.

Then two hundred grams of flour... no, sorry, one-ninety, a teaspoon of baking soda, and a pinch of salt. Fold it, don't overmix.

Optional, a handful of chopped walnuts. Pour into a lined loaf tin and bake until a skewer comes out clean.
