# Kanda Poha

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Poha, breakfast ka. Do cup poha chhalni mein dho ke side rakh do, bas ek baar, zyada nahi warna mushy ho jaata hai.

Kadhai mein do tablespoon oil, ek teaspoon rai, ek mutthi moongphali. Phir ek onion chopped, do teen hari mirch, aur das kadi patte.

Onion soft ho jaaye to half teaspoon haldi, poha, namak, aur ek teaspoon sugar. Mix karke dhak do do minute.

Upar se nimbu, thoda sa, aur dhaniya. Sev ho toh perfect.
