# Double ka Meetha

Read it out loud the way you'd explain it to someone on the phone. Pauses, "umm", and small changes to the in-between words are fine. **Say every ingredient, every amount, and every correction exactly as written**, including the mistakes: they're there on purpose.

## Say this

Double ka meetha, meetha hai bread ka. Aath bread ke slice lo, kinare kaat ke tikon mein kaat lo.

Kadhai mein chaar bade chammach ghee, aur slice dono taraf se sunehre tal lo.

Chaashni ke liye ek cup cheeni aur aadha cup paani, ek taar ki chaashni. Usme chaar elaichi kooti hui aur thoda sa kesar.

Doodh, aadha litre... nahi, saat sau ml doodh, ubaal ke aadha kar lo.

Ab bread ko chaashni mein dubo ke tray mein lagao, upar se gaadha doodh daal do. Kaju aur badaam ke tukde upar se. Thanda karke parosna.
